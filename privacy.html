Privacy Policy

My Health Data · Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. Who we are

My Health Data ("the app", App ID 119208391) is a personal, non-commercial tool built and used by an individual developer, Elaraby Elaidy, to analyze their own health and fitness data recorded by their Huawei wearable device. The app is not published in any app store and is not offered to the public.

2. What data we access

With the user's explicit authorization through HUAWEI ID and Huawei Health Kit, the app reads the following data types only:

  • Step count and daily activity
  • Heart rate
  • Sleep records
  • Exercise / workout records
  • Blood oxygen saturation (SpO2)
  • Stress level

The app only reads data. It never writes, modifies or deletes any data in Huawei Health.

3. How we use the data

The data is used only to produce personal statistics and charts (daily, weekly, monthly and yearly trends) so the user can understand their own activity, sleep and heart health. The app does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

4. Where the data is stored

Data is downloaded over an encrypted HTTPS connection and stored only on the user's own personal computer. It is not uploaded to any server, cloud service or database operated by the developer or any third party.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose the data to any third party. The data is not used for advertising, profiling or any commercial purpose.

6. Retention and deletion

The user can delete the locally stored data at any time by deleting the files on their computer. The user can revoke the app's access at any time in the Huawei Health app or HUAWEI ID account settings, after which the app can no longer read any data.

7. Security

Access tokens and app credentials are stored locally and are never published or shared. All communication with Huawei services uses HTTPS.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children and does not knowingly process children's data.

9. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue at github.com/elarabyelaidy19/healthdata-legal/issues.
